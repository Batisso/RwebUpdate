FAQs

Common Questions

1
How do I start a project with Rendo Creatives?

Simply reach out to us via email or phone to discuss your project requirements. We'll guide you through the process from start to finish.

2
What industries do you specialize in?

We work with a variety of industries, including tech, healthcare, retail, and more. Our versatile team can adapt to your specific needs.

3
How do you ensure quality in your services?

Our team follows robust quality assurance processes and uses the latest technologies to ensure the highest standards in all our projects.

4
How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines typically range from 2 to 6 weeks, depending on complexity. Smaller automation systems can often be deployed within 2–3 weeks, while more advanced projects may take 4–6 weeks or longer.

5
Do you offer post-launch support and maintenance?

Yes, we provide ongoing support and maintenance packages to ensure your digital solutions continue to perform optimally as your business grows.

6
Can you help with redesigning an existing website?

Absolutely. We can audit your current site and provide a comprehensive redesign that improves user experience, performance, and conversion rates.

7
What is AI Automation and how can it benefit my business?

AI Automation uses intelligent software to handle repetitive tasks and complex data processing. It increases efficiency, reduces human error, and frees up your team for high-value strategic work.

8
Is my data secure when using AI solutions?

Yes, data security is our top priority. We implement 24/7 Autonomous Operation and follow industry best practices to ensure your data is protected and used ethically within all AI integrations.

9
Can AI replace my current employees?

AI is designed to augment and empower your team, not replace them. It handles repetitive tasks, allowing your employees to focus on creativity, strategy, and customer relationships.

10
What kind of ROI can I expect from AI implementation?

Most businesses see significant ROI through reduced operational costs, faster response times (especially with AI chatbots), and improved data-driven decision-making.
